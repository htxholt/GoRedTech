About RedTech

Direct engineering expertise across the systems that keep work moving.

RedTech is a Houston-based, remote-ready consultancy for organizations that need practical diagnosis, hands-on implementation, and clear accountability from the engineer doing the work.

Cross-disciplinary background

Fourteen years connecting application support, automation, infrastructure, data, and the web.

The background spans major incident and P1 response, business-critical application support, RPA and workflow automation, APIs and integrations, Linux and Windows, scripting and programming, SQL and NoSQL, Docker and self-hosted infrastructure, networking, identity and TLS, cloud and virtualization, web systems, documentation, and training.

That range matters when an operational problem does not respect vendor or discipline boundaries. RedTech can trace the workflow across systems, identify the actual constraint, and keep the recommendation grounded in what the business can operate.

What to expect
  • Direct access to the engineer doing the work
  • Recommendations tied to an operating outcome
  • Plain-language tradeoffs, scope, and assumptions
  • Security and recovery considered in the design
  • Verification against the real workflow
  • Documentation that survives handoff
Operating principles

How RedTech approaches technical decisions.

01

Diagnose before prescribing

Understand the users, workflow, evidence, constraints, dependencies, and failure modes before selecting a tool.

02

Scope what can be verified

Define the target outcome, assumptions, boundaries, and acceptance criteria before expensive work begins.

03

Prefer durable systems

Favor maintainability, security, clear ownership, observable health, and recoverable operation over novelty.

04

Keep communication direct

Explain what is changing, why it matters, what remains uncertain, and who owns the next action.

05

Build for the operator

Use the smallest supportable solution that fits the team, environment, and real workload.

06

Document the handoff

Leave operating, recovery, dependency, and next-step information where the business can use it.
