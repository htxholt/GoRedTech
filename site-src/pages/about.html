About RedTech

Direct technical expertise. Clear accountability.

RedTech is a Houston-based IT consultancy for organizations that want capable engineering, practical recommendations, and a partner who stays close to the work.

Our position

Technology should reduce friction—not create another layer of it.

Businesses often inherit disconnected systems, unclear ownership, inconsistent vendors, and tools that no longer match the work. RedTech brings those pieces into one picture and turns that picture into a plan.

When you work with RedTech, you work directly with the engineer designing and deploying your systems. That keeps decisions grounded, communication clear, and implementation accountable.

What you can expect
  • Recommendations tied to business outcomes
  • Plain-language tradeoffs and priorities
  • Security considered from the beginning
  • Documentation that does not disappear at handoff
  • A solution sized for your actual operation
