Systems reliability

Cross-service startup validation

Problem
Services failed during startup even though individual components appeared correctly configured.
Approach
Diagnosed a cross-service startup failure caused by DNS readiness and boot ordering, then added health-based validation.
Result
The startup path became dependency-aware and verifiable instead of relying on timing assumptions.
Working standard

Understand the failure mode, build the control, verify the real path.

The technology changes by engagement. The standard stays consistent: evidence before assumptions, clear scope, supportable implementation, health-based verification where appropriate, and documented handoff.
